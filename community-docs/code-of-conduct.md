# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want Rahat's community to be open, welcoming, friendly, and inclusive. We are committed to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

## Our Standards

Members of this community are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate in all communications.
- Refrain from demeaning, discriminatory, or harassing behavior and speech.
- Assume good faith and be welcoming to newcomers.
- Be mindful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and receive constructive feedback graciously.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are examples of unacceptable behavior and will not be tolerated:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Threats of violence or advocacy of harm against any person or group.
- Insults, name-calling, or derogatory comments about a person's identity.
- Public shaming or posting private information (doxing).
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or the community’s processes.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, including but not limited to:

- Issue trackers, pull requests, and code review comments.
- Mailing lists, chat channels, and forums.
- Community events (virtual and in-person).

It also applies to behavior by community members when they are representing the project, including at conferences, meetups, and online.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it. You can report incidents by contacting the project maintainers at `team@rahat.io` 
When reporting, please include as much of the following as possible:

- A description of the incident and the behavior observed.
- The date, time, and location (link) where it occurred.
- The names of people involved and any witnesses.
- Screenshots, logs, or copies of messages (if applicable).


## Confidentiality & Protection

We prioritize the safety and privacy of reporters. Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent possible. Retaliation against anyone who reports a problem in good faith will not be tolerated.

## Responsibility

All community members are encouraged to help foster a positive environment. Maintainers are responsible for the enforcement of this policy and for responding to reports promptly and fairly.
